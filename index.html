Introduction

Tulus is an Indonesian singer and songwriter known for his warm vocals, thoughtful lyrics, and elegant musical style.

Musical Debut

He began his professional music career with his self-titled debut album Tulus, released in 2011.

Independent Career

Tulus developed his career independently through his own music label and creative direction, TulusCompany.

Musical Style

His music combines pop, soul, and jazz influences with personal storytelling and poetic lyrics.
